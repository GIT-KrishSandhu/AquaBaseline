# AquaBaseline: 60-Second Video Narration Script

**Project:** AquaBaseline: Smart Water Monitoring That Learns What "Normal" Looks Like  
**Author:** Krish Sandhu (KIET Group of Institutions, Uttar Pradesh)  
**Program:** 1M1B Internships on Green Skills and Applied AI for Climate Action  
**Format:** First-person student voice, clear cadence, ~150 words total, timed to 60 seconds.

---

### Script with Timestamps

| Timestamp | Video Section | Spoken Narration (First Person) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00 – 0:08** | **The Problem (Hook)** | "Campuses track water as a single monthly total, which means a 1,000-litre-per-hour pipe leak running at 3 AM is completely invisible on the utility bill." |
| **0:08 – 0:20** | **The Research Discovery** | "When I analysed 500 country-year records, I found that variation inside a single building over time is larger than the gap between buildings. A blunt, fixed campus threshold fails to catch hidden faults." |
| **0:20 – 0:34** | **The Solution & Injection** | "So I built AquaBaseline. It meters water every 15 minutes, normalizes by headcount, and uses an hour-aware Isolation Forest to learn each building's normal diurnal rhythm. Here in the Scenario Lab, when we inject an overnight pipe leak..." |
| **0:34 – 0:45** | **Alert & Feedback Loop** | "...the model immediately flags it as an Alert with a plain-English explanation. Maintenance staff can log whether it's a real leak or a false alarm, retraining the model in real time." |
| **0:45 – 0:54** | **Empirical Results** | "In 20-seed validation tests, our model caught 80 to 85% of leak nights with under 2.5% false alerts—while traditional daily threshold rules missed 100% of them. On our 5,000-person campus, a 10% cut saves 50 million litres every year." |
| **0:54 – 1:00** | **The Next Step** | "Next, I'm working to fit physical pulse meters on three campus buildings and test directly with our plumbing crew. Thank you." |
